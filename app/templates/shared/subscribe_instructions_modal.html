How to Subscribe to Your Calendar Feed

Copy your unique subscription URL from your calendar card, then follow the instructions for your calendar provider:

  1. Open Google Calendar on the web (calendar.google.com).
  2. On the left sidebar, click the "+" button next to "Other calendars".
  3. Select "From URL" from the menu.
  4. Paste your copied CalendarSync subscription URL into the "URL of calendar" field.
  5. Click "Add calendar".
  1. Open Apple Calendar on your Mac, iPhone, or iPad.
  2. On Mac: Go to File > New Calendar Subscription. On iOS: Go to Settings > Calendar > Accounts > Add Account > Other > Add Subscribed Calendar.
  3. Paste your copied CalendarSync subscription URL.
  4. Click Subscribe and set auto-refresh (e.g., Every hour).
  1. Open Outlook on the Web (outlook.live.com / outlook.office.com).
  2. Navigate to the Calendar view.
  3. Click "Add calendar" in the left pane, then select "Subscribe from web".
  4. Paste your copied subscription URL, enter a calendar name, and click Import.
